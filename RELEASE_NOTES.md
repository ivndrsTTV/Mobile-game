Download **Voltfall.apk** on your Android phone and open it to install. It installs over v1.0.0 or later and keeps your saves.

**Coming from an APK older than v1.0.0?** Copy your progress first with **Save slots → Backup code → Show my code → Copy**, uninstall the old game, install this one, and paste the code under **Restore from code**.

## What's new in v1.2.0

### Volt Clash: a smarter, less predictable horde
- **Eight new horde-only cards**, never seen in missions:
  - **Crawlers**: four tiny, quick infected for 1 energy.
  - **Hunter**: leaps onto its first target and lands a heavy blow.
  - **Riot Zombie**: armored, takes 40% less damage.
  - **Screamer**: makes nearby infected faster and stronger.
  - **Plague Priest**: raises two walkers every few seconds.
  - **Abomination**: the biggest tank of all; goes straight for your towers.
  - **Plague Cloud**: a poison spell that hurts and slows.
  - **Rising Dead**: walkers climb out of the ground anywhere, even on your side.
- **A new horde deck every battle.** The horde draws 8 of its 16 cards at random from the ones its tier has unlocked: always a win condition, a spell and a swarm, the rest random. No single counter works every time.
- A scouting report shows which cards it brought (also in the pause menu), a label shows each card it plays, and the Volt Clash screen has a bestiary of all the horde's cards.
- The horde follows its tanks with support troops and drops Rising Dead on towers that are already hurting.
- Difficulty climbs more smoothly across the ten opponents.

### Fixes
- A boss could spawn inside a wall in Raijin Bunker and The Hive, making "Kill the boss" impossible. Bosses now always spawn where you can reach them.
- A rescue mission where too many survivors died could never be won. It now ends with "Too many survivors were lost", so you can retry.
- Messages no longer hide behind the card bar in Volt Clash.
