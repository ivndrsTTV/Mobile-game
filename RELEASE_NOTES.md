## What's new in v1.4.0

### Gems and Style
- **Gems** are a new currency used only for looks. They never make you stronger, and God Mode can't make them.
- **Style screen** (on the title screen) with a live preview of each heroine:
  - **Skins:** six colorways per heroine (Midnight, Sakura, Snow Queen, Crimson, Volt Gold, Toxic). 120 gems each.
  - **Dye kit:** pick any of 14 hair and outfit colors for one heroine. 200 gems.
  - **Attack color:** seven colors for her attacks and glow. 80 gems.
  - **Attack style:** hearts, stars or petals. 120 gems.
  - **Aura:** Volt, Sakura, Frost, Inferno, Void or Gold. 150 gems.
  - **Trail:** sparkles, petals, embers or snow. 100 gems.
  - **Animation:** hover, bounce or sway. 100 gems.
  - Skins and dye kits are per heroine. Everything else is unlocked once and works on any heroine.
- **Gem Shop:** 120 gems for $0.99, 650 for $4.99, 1400 for $9.99, 3000 for $19.99.

### Two editions
- **Private edition:** God Mode is already owned and shown as $5.99. Gem packs are free test purchases; no money is involved.
- **Public edition:** God Mode shows a $5.99 price and gem packs show their prices, but the store isn't open yet. Nothing can be bought and no payment system is included. Backup codes from the private edition don't bring gems, cosmetics or God Mode into it.

## What's new in v1.3.0

### Volt Clash: a fair fight
- **Same energy for both sides.** The horde now earns energy at exactly your rate and can't spend what it doesn't have. Its higher tiers are stronger cards, not more energy.
- **Every heroine has a real ability.** Each heroine card casts her own signature move a few seconds after she reaches the fight, then again on a cooldown. Some examples: Raiko's chain lightning, Kurenai's clones, Shiden's railgun, Rei's freeze, Misaki's heal. A gold bar under her shows when it's charging, and the card says what it does.
- **Installations work like defenses should.** They don't move, have lots of HP, hit hard for their cost, and wear down over about a minute.
  - **Turret:** now 3 energy (was 4), with 70% more HP, 70% more damage and twice the lifetime.
  - **New: Tesla Coil.** Its lightning arcs to nearby enemies.
  - **New: Mortar.** Long range splash, but it can't hit enemies right next to it.
  - **New: Barracks.** Sends out rifle troops on a timer.
  - **New: Energy Pump.** Makes extra energy while it stands.
- **The horde builds too:** **Spine Tower** (a defense) and **Brood Sac** (its own pump).
- **Troops can be hit while they deploy.** Answer a drop before it gets going.
- **Take a tower, take its lane.** Destroy a princess tower and you can deploy on the enemy side of that lane (shown in pink). The horde can do the same to you.
- **Crowns.** Each princess tower is a crown that rises from the rubble. The king tower is worth all three.
- **A real end screen.** It shows both sides' crowns and your rewards, with **Play again**, **Next opponent** and **Menu** buttons.
- **More life on the field.**
  - Tower guards face the enemy and move while they wait.
  - Your heroine stands on your king tower.
  - The infected climb out of the ground when they deploy, lunge when they attack and flinch when hit.
  - Stuns, shields, haste and freeze are now visible.
